Axon

A personal Gmail assistant operated by Andreas Iskrov for the owner and a small number of personally known users.

What it does

Axon connects to Gmail with your permission to search and read messages, detect incoming mail, and create drafts. Email content can be processed by the configured AI service to answer questions and assist with drafting.

The Gmail module requests read-only and compose permissions. Google's compose permission also permits sending, although this module exposes draft creation rather than a send tool.

This is a private personal integration, not a public signup service. Connecting a Gmail account requires the owner's arrangement and the account holder's consent.
