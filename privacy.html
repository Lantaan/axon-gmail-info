Gmail module homepage

Privacy policy

Updated 9 October 2026. This policy covers Andreas Iskrov’s personal Google OAuth deployment of his Gmail module for the Axon framework, exclusively for his own use.

If you are not Andreas Iskrov and are reading this, please head back; this project is not available for your use.

Access and purpose

With the owner’s Google consent, the module accesses his Gmail account identifier, message and thread identifiers, headers and message contents to search and read mail, detect incoming messages and create drafts. Attachments supplied for drafts are uploaded to Gmail. The scopes are gmail.readonly and gmail.compose; Google defines compose as also permitting sending, although the module exposes no send tool.

AI processing

Gmail content returned to the assistant can be included in its conversation and sent to the configured AI provider for inference. The current deployment uses OpenAI through Codex. Provider processing and retention depend on the service terms and account settings; zero provider retention is not promised. Gmail data is used for the owner’s requested assistant functions, not sold or used for advertising, and must not be used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and listener state are stored on the computer running the module. Message content may persist in local conversation records, logs, saved outputs and provider records. Tokens and mailbox data must be kept private and are not published on this website or in its repository. Protection depends on the operator’s computer security and service account permissions.

Retention and removal

There is no automatic local deletion schedule. The owner can remove local credentials, listener state, conversation records and saved outputs. Provider-side deletion is subject to provider controls and retention rules. Future Gmail access can be revoked through Google Account connections; revocation alone does not erase saved records. Created Gmail drafts remain until removed.

Website hosting

This static informational website does not connect to Gmail or collect mailbox data and has no application analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may record visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Google Limited Use

This integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
